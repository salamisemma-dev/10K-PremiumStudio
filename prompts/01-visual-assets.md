# Prompt Blueprint: Visual Assets

## Goal

Generate a coherent asset plan for the five-asset rule.

## Master Prompt

Generate a photorealistic premium asset for `[PRODUCT OR BRAND]`.

Setting: `[CINEMATIC SCENE]`.

Lighting: match the scene exactly.

Perspective: choose one of these:

- Wide hero shot.
- Extreme texture close-up.
- Detail shot for proof.
- Break-apart product render.

Style: minimal composition, realistic materials, no distracting background objects.

## Manifest Requirement

For every planned asset, record:

- Role.
- Source or generation prompt.
- Final URL or storage location.
- Width.
- Height.
- Alt text.
- License or ownership note.
- Load strategy.
