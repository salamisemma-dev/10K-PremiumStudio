10K-PremiumStudio concept selector

Three landing page directions

This file is a decision aid, not a final website. Replace the placeholders with one client brief, compare three directions, choose one, then record the selected template and rejected runner-up in brief.md.

Canonical six fields

  • klantnaam[Client or brand name]
  • one thing[Single promise the page must prove]
  • scene[Cinematic setting, light, material, mood]
  • proof[Evidence, product detail, case, demo, result]
  • assets[Five scene-derived assets, no stock dependency]
  • CTA[One primary action]

Scene wins. The template shapes the story; the scene sets palette, typography mood, light, and motion restraint.

Concept A

Cinematic Signature

A full-scene brand opening for a client whose atmosphere is the strongest proof of premium value.

Selected template
hero-immersive
Rejected runner-up
narrative-driven, because the first screen should persuade before the story unfolds.
Layout rhythm
One dominant hero, slow reveal, four proof tiles, quiet close.
Brand direction
Warm cinematic contrast, restrained logo presence, large confident type, minimal UI chrome.
Asset plan
Hero scene, two detail crops, one proof image, one SVG mark.
CTA direction
Single invitation tied to the premium promise.
Why this fits
The one thing is felt through the scene first; copy only sharpens the signal.

Choose when: the brand needs instant desire before explanation.

Concept B

Proof In Motion

A video-led or spatial reveal route for products, venues, or services that need motion to become believable.

Selected template
video-3d-reveal
Rejected runner-up
product-first, because the proof depends on movement rather than static variants.
Layout rhythm
Hero frame, spatial reveal, proof orbit, mechanism slice, still close.
Brand direction
Precise studio light, technical calm, tactile surfaces, measured depth cues.
Asset plan
Hero video with poster, product still, two proof details, one SVG mark.
CTA direction
Book a demo, request a private viewing, or start the configuration.
Why this fits
The one thing becomes credible only when the user sees the object or system move.

Choose when: motion is proof, not decoration.

Concept C

Authority System

A trust-first direction for experts, consultancies, or high-ticket services where method and proof must lead.

Selected template
consultant-authority
Rejected runner-up
service-agency, because personal authority is stronger than a broad service menu.
Layout rhythm
Thesis, proof ladder, method, results, focused CTA.
Brand direction
Quiet editorial spacing, sober contrast, precise language, credible restraint.
Asset plan
Portrait or work scene, method diagram, two proof visuals, one SVG mark.
CTA direction
Apply, schedule intake, or request a strategic review.
Why this fits
The one thing is a claim of judgment; the page must make the expert feel inevitable.

Choose when: trust, taste, and track record carry the sale.

How to decide

Pick one route before build

Do not merge concepts. Choose the direction whose template, proof strategy, and brand direction best serve the same scene.

  3. Record selected template, rejected runner-up, and one reason in brief.md.
