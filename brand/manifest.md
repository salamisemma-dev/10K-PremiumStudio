# Brand Manifest: 10K-PremiumStudio

## Identity

10K-PremiumStudio builds calm, cinematic landing-page-led websites for premium offers.

We do not make loud templates. We direct one focused digital scene.

## Philosophy

One scene. Five assets. Built with restraint.

A premium website earns attention through space, precision, and timing. It does not fill every gap.

Cinematic restraint means controlled pacing, material confidence, and quiet authority. Reference sites may inform composition and restraint, but the scene decides and copying is forbidden.

## Tone Of Voice

- Calm.
- Reduced.
- Specific.
- No exclamation marks.
- No em dash characters.
- No vague superlatives.
- Short sentences.
- Facts before adjectives.

## Design Laws

1. Use no more than five hero assets per website: two videos and three images.
2. All assets share one cinematic lighting strategy.
3. Color comes from the scene, not from random hex choices.
4. White space is a primary design material.
5. Typography uses contrast: large headlines, small labels, quiet body text.
6. Familiar interaction patterns are allowed when they improve clarity, accessibility, or conversion.

## Technical Standard

- Semantic HTML.
- Tailwind CSS.
- GSAP for measured motion.
- Optional Three.js when 3D has a real purpose.
- Schema markup.
- Accessible media.
- Fixed media dimensions.
- Reduced-motion fallback.
- Local quality target: 95 or higher for Lighthouse categories where tooling is available.

## AI Operating Rule

Build only when the canonical six fields are present or explicitly assumed in the client brief:

- `klantnaam`
- `one thing`
- `scene`
- `proof`
- `assets`
